## Metric and date definitions

- Totals count current non-archived records in the selected scope. With dates, companies/contacts/leads/opportunities/tasks use **creation timestamps**; activities use **occurred_at**. `from` is inclusive at UTC midnight; `through` includes that entire UTC calendar day by using an exclusive next-midnight bound. Either bound may be omitted; reversed/invalid/out-of-range dates are rejected.
- Status/stage counts are the **current status of the creation cohort**, not status history or a time-series change rate. Lead qualification share is currently QUALIFIED / leads in that cohort. It is not lead-to-opportunity conversion, which lacks an authoritative link/history contract.
- Closed-deal win share is currently CLOSED_WON / (CLOSED_WON + CLOSED_LOST) within the cohort. Zero denominators display “Not applicable.” Opportunity amounts, including won values, are not recognized revenue.
- Open tasks are OPEN or IN_PROGRESS. Overdue tasks are those pending tasks with due_at at/before the snapshot instant. Completion share is currently COMPLETED / tasks in the creation cohort. **Completed in period** independently counts currently COMPLETED tasks whose completed_at falls in the chosen dates, and therefore can exceed the count of tasks created during those dates. Reopened tasks no longer contribute to that current completion metric; completion history is not fabricated.
- Owner/member filtering applies to opportunity owner and task assignee only. Pipeline/currency/search filters apply to opportunities only. Other entity totals retain their date scope. Dashboard uses an unfiltered current snapshot; reports and pipeline store their filters in URLs for Back/Forward and direct links.
- All-pipeline visualization uses existing compatibility outcome stages. Selecting a configured pipeline shows its actual ordered stable stage keys/names. Moving a legacy opportunity into a configured pipeline is explicit; terminal stages cannot reopen. Stage filtering narrows the card page; the stage aggregate columns describe the broader search/currency/owner/pipeline scope.

## Integrity, limits and security

Global counts and stage/currency totals are SQL aggregates, not sums of a capped client list. Full currency groups are separate from bounded custom pipeline/team sections. Custom pipeline groups are capped at 2,000; owner and assignee groups at 200 each. Each response exposes truncation flags, UI discloses partial group lists, configured board hides incomplete stage totals, and CSV contains truncation rows. Users can refine by pipeline/member; bounded group lists are never presented as full organization totals. Cards are one page of 25; search shows 10 per resource; recent activity shows 10. These limits are disclosed.

Every reporting service entry enforces persisted active membership through existing `crmAuthorization.canView`; queries bind organization ID and dates/filter values. VIEWER is permitted to read/export authorized aggregate information, while writes still require OWNER/ADMIN/SALES_USER and fresh CSRF. Notification counts/pages are recipient scoped. No API header or browser role grants access independently. A foreign filter ID yields no matching tenant records; it does not disclose foreign records or member names.

Report CSV quotes all cells, doubles embedded quotes, preserves CRLF/newlines and Unicode, guards spreadsheet formula prefixes, and preserves exact amounts/currency. Columns include snapshot time, UTC dates and applied member/pipeline/currency/search scope. No customer record collection or unrelated tenant name is embedded in aggregate exports. No migration is needed: all aggregation fields/indexes already exist.

## Remaining scope and release limits

True lead-to-opportunity conversion, historical trends/time-series stage changes, recognized revenue, revenue forecasts and historical completion/reopen metrics remain unavailable until an approved data contract exists. The UI identifies these limits; it does not invent data. Pilot latency/load targets, full WCAG 2.2 AA certification, deployed TLS/cookie/proxy acceptance, approved recovery/invite delivery, branch/security scan policies and backup/restore remain the Phase 1/2 release backlog. No production readiness or autonomous/AI feature claim is made.
