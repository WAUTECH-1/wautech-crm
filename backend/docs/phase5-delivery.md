## Authorization, consent and privacy

All reads require persisted enabled user, active organization membership and canView. Shared profile changes require canManageOrganization (OWNER/ADMIN). Goals/milestone changes and task preview/confirmation require canWrite (OWNER/ADMIN/SALES_USER). Every member, including VIEWER, may correct/delete only their own onboarding/preferences; their identity is derived server-side and never accepted from the request body. Private answers are not exposed to organization administrators through these APIs.

Every mutation uses existing session-cookie security and fresh CSRF. Numeric versions implement optimistic conflict detection. Concurrent changes return 409 instead of overwriting. Operational previews also lock/recheck the goal, revalidate existing TaskService authorization and tenant parent relationships, and flush task persistence before attaching its composite foreign key. A failed confirmation consumes its preview and requires fresh review; ambiguous retries must refresh the goal before making another preview.

Storage consent and AI disclosure are separate. OWNER/ADMIN can disable organization business sharing; each user can revoke their private sharing. A per-question confirmation is required even after both saved opt-ins. No provider credential exists in frontend code. No website scraping, microphone capture, hidden background recording, new third-party speech calls or autonomous operations occur.

Profile/preference deletion physically removes the active database row; goal deletion removes its milestones/planning links while retaining existing operational CRM tasks. Corrections, deletion and sharing revocation invalidate copied business context in registered server AI sessions. In-flight obsolete responses are discarded. Existing browser snapshots may remain visible until refresh/navigation/conversation expiry; no private answers are saved in browser local/session storage. Provider copies already transmitted follow provider retention; local deletion cannot promise their immediate erasure. Existing Phase 4 history remains bounded to 20 minutes plus minute cleanup grace. Task previews expire in five minutes with minute cleanup. Audit IDs and durable AI budget counters are retained under existing operational policies, without deleted business content.

These are the product retention boundaries for this slice, not a certification of regulatory compliance. Disabled/archived membership data erasure or exported/backup copies require the existing administrative retention/recovery process. Customer deployment, backup, complete accessibility and performance release gates remain open.

## Fact and interpretation boundary

- Shared profile and private preferences: user-provided facts; can be corrected/deleted, and are not independently verified.
- CRM summary: current tenant SQL aggregates with Phase 3 definitions and exact separate currencies.
- Goals: real persisted planning records with user-declared milestone progress. A COMPLETED goal does not prove revenue, business success or task completion.
- AI suggestions: unverified hypotheses/recommendations with permitted evidence references and explicit limitations. They are not persisted as factual profile attributes and never automatically create goals/tasks. Users review/edit goal plans and confirm actual operational creation.

Prior conversation turns are still not retransmitted. Questions should identify the relevant planning context. No forecast, business guarantee, psychological pressure or validated prediction claim is made. Provider safety/factuality on actual models remains unverified.

## Production, experimental and roadmap classification

The implemented business CRUD/consent/goal-to-task foundation is integrated and testable in the production frontend; deployed pilot readiness is not claimed solely from local/CI tests. The advisory integration is implemented but actual model behavior/live AI remains unverified. Text is the complete interaction alternative.

Voice: design/verification gate only; no production capture layer is enabled. Browser SpeechRecognition has limited compatibility and may transmit audio to a recognition service, and there is no configured approved speech provider or reliable microphone fixture. See [voice/spatial foundation](./voice-spatial-foundation.md).

Spatial/WebXR/headset/Vision Pro: technical design and prototype milestones only. No 3D engine, spatial navigation or untested opt-in prototype is shipped into production. Same authorized REST contracts are defined in [business data contracts](./business-data-contracts.md).

Remaining: approve/configure/live-evaluate the provider; hardware-tested opt-in voice and speech retention, real headset/accessibility/performance trials, wider planning/invitation/recovery rollout. Automatic task-state synchronization, financial projections, website scraping and autonomous external actions are outside this approved foundation.
